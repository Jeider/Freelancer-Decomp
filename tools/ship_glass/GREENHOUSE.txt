Greenhouse glazing profile
==========================
RPSurfaceGreenhouse is selected in Shading only for the blended material named
space_girder01_op. Standard ship glass retains its separate ~33% opacity.
The original texture alpha distinguishes metal ribs from glass panes:
- nearly opaque ribs keep their texture and direct lighting;
- pane tint, reflection and optical density use 1.34x the first preset
  (33% less than the doubled preset);
- panes use pale neutral grey; environmental reflection keeps only 20% chroma;
- ~37% pane opacity face-on, increasing at grazing angles with reflections;
- original UVs, textures, geometry, sorting and two-sided submission are retained.
No scene refraction, bloom pass, additional map, or animated procedural noise.

Inspected installed SOLAR models using this material:
DOCKABLE/freeport7.cmp
MISC/space_dome.cmp, space_domea.3db, space_domeb.3db, space_dome_alt.cmp
MISC/space_dome_dmg.cmp, space_dome_dmg2.cmp
MISC/RMBASE/space_dome.cmp, space_dome2.cmp

space_large_dome.3db uses a different opaque space_girder01special material.
It is intentionally not reclassified as transparent by this profile: that needs
an asset/sorting change, not just a pixel-shader override.

Checks: ship_glass/check.py tests actual SM3 tint/opacity/solid ribs, edge response
and unchanged ship glass. player_shadow_quality/check.py checks production RP9
profile selection and reset, alongside the existing nearby-station shadow tests.
These GPU fixtures do not replace visual confirmation on the actual station.
